# JobFlow Dashboard — Locked 4–2–3 Layout

## Row 1 — Executive KPIs
1. Total Revenue
2. Jobs This Month
3. New Customer
4. Outstanding Invoices (Amount)

## Row 2 — Operational Tables
1. Recent Jobs
2. Recent Invoices

## Row 3 — Analytics
1. Revenue by Service
2. Jobs by Staff
3. Job Status

The dashboard intentionally avoids adding additional KPI/card rows. Financial detail beyond the executive outstanding-invoice metric belongs in Finance / Accounts Receivable.

## Data scope
- Revenue is the sum of completed jobs created during the current calendar month.
- Jobs This Month counts non-archived jobs created during the current calendar month.
- New Customer counts non-archived customers created during the current calendar month.
- Outstanding Invoices is the positive balance due on active receivable invoices in the Business currency.
- Revenue by Service is monthly completed-job value grouped by service.
- Jobs by Staff is monthly job-assignment volume.
- Job Status is the current status distribution across non-archived jobs.
